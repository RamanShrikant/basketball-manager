BM Patch 39D - Team Intel Untouchable + Target Caps

Purpose:
- Tightens visible untouchables so useful young players are not automatically labeled untouchable.
- Keeps internal protection for real franchise/core pieces while allowing more realistic availability elsewhere.
- Rebuilding/retooling visible untouchables now require true franchise upside, not just 75-80 OVR young talent.
- Contenders can show true stars/young title core as untouchable: Maxey/Brown/Sengun/Barnes/Harper/Castle-type cases can qualify.
- Older stars on non-contenders are generally not visible untouchables.
- Targets now enforce max one target from each source team per buyer team.
- Targets now enforce max three appearances per player across the whole Team Intel page.
- Displayed untouchable, trade block, targets, and expiring lists remain sorted highest OVR to lowest OVR after selection.
